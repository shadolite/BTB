 $\quad$ <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.1|1]]**<span class="endversemarker">]</span> Now these are the names of the children of Israel who dwelt in Egypt, who had come with Jacob, every man with his family came all the sons of Jacob unto Egypt.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.2|2]]**<span class="endversemarker">]The children of Leah: Reuben, and Simeon, and Levi, and Judah, and Issachar, and Zebulun, and Dinah, their sister. 
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.3|3]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And the sons of Rachel: Joseph and Benjamin.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.4|4]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And the sons of Zilpah, the handmaid of Leah: Gad and Asher.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.5|5]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And the sons of Bilhah, the handmaid of Rachel: Dan and Naphtali.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.6|6]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And these are their generations that were born unto them in the land of Canaan, before they came unto Egypt with Jacob their father.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.7|7]]**<span class="endversemarker">]The sons of Reuben: Chanoch, and Pallu, and Chetzron and Carmi.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.8|8]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And the sons of Simeon: Jemuel, and Jamin, and Ohad, and Jachin, and Zochar and Saul, the son of the Canaanitish woman.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.9|9]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And the children of Levi: Gershon, and Kehath and Merari, and their sister Jochebed, who was born unto them in going down to Egypt.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.10|10]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And the sons of Judah: Er, and Onan, and Shelah, and Herez and Zarach.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.11|11]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan; and the sons of Perez were Chezron and Chamul.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.12|12]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And the sons of Issachar: Tola, and Puvah, and Job and Shomron.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.13|13]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And the sons of Zebulun: Sered, and Elon and Jachleel, and the son of Dan was Chushim.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.14|14]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And the sons of Naphtali: Jachzeel, and Guni, and Jetzer and Shilam.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.15|15]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And the sons of Gad: Ziphion, and Chaggi, and Shuni, and Ezbon, and Eri, and Arodi and Areli.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.16|16]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And the children of Asher: Jim nah, and Jishvah, and Jishvi, and Beriah and Serach, their sister. And the sons of Beriah: Cheber and Malchiel.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.17|17]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And the sons of Benjamin: Bela, and Becher, and Ashbel, and Gera, and Naaman, and Achi, and Rosh, and Mupim, and Chupim and Ord.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.18|18]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And the sons of Joseph, that had been born unto him in Egypt were: Manasseh and Ephraim.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.19|19]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And all the souls that came out of the loins of Jacob were seventy souls, those that had come with Jacob their father to dwell there. And Joseph and all his brothers dwelt in Egypt securely, and they ate the best of the land in Egypt, through all the days of Joseph’s life;

<!-- <big><big>[[BoEx.1.20 Joseph Dies - Egypt Weeps for 70 Days|⥃]]</big></big>***Joseph Dies - Egypt Weeps for 70 Days***&emsp;<small><sup>\[ [[Acts.7|Acts 7:15–19]] ]</sup></small> -->

 $\quad$ <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.|]]**<span class="endversemarker">]and Joseph lived in the land of Egypt for ninety-three years, and he ruled over all Egypt eighty years.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.20|20]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And when the days of Joseph drew nigh that he had to die, he sent and called his brothers and all the household of his father, and they all came together and they seated themselves before him,
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.21|21]]**<span class="endversemarker">]and Joseph said unto his brothers, and all the household of his father: Behold, I die, and God will surely visit you, and he will bring you up into the land which he hath sworn to your fathers to give it unto them.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.22|22]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And when the Lord will visit you to bring you up from this land into the land of your fathers, then carry ye up my bones with you from here.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.23|23]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And Joseph took an oath from the children of Israel, saying: God will surely visit you, and ye shall then carry up my bones from here;
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.24|24]]**<span class="endversemarker">]and after this Joseph died in that year, the seventy-first year of their going down to Egypt.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.25|25]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And Joseph was a hundred and ten years old when he died in the land of Egypt, and all his brothers and all his servants took him and they embalmed Joseph according to their custom, and his brothers and all Egypt wept over him for seventy days.
 <span class="startversemarker">[</span>**[[BoEx.1.26|26]]**<span class="endversemarker">]And they placed Joseph into a coffin filled with spices and all kinds of perfumery, and they buried him on the side of the river Thiebor, and his sons and all his brothers, and the entire house hold of his father, made for him a mourning of seven days.
